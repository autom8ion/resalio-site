Resalio Privacy Policy

Effective September 23, 2026

Resalio ("the app") estimates the resale value of items you photograph. This policy explains what data the app handles and how.

What we collect

  • Photos you choose to scan. A photo is uploaded only when you tap the shutter or pick an image from your library. We do not access your camera roll otherwise.
  • Optional text hints you type to help identify an item.
  • Scan results (item identification, estimated value, comparable listings) so you can see your history.
  • An anonymous account identifier. The app creates a random account on first launch. No name, email, or phone number is required.
  • Subscription status. Purchases are processed by Apple or Google and managed through RevenueCat. We receive an anonymized purchase record, never your payment details.
  • Waitlist email. If you join the waitlist on this site, we store your email address and, optionally, where you sell, solely to tell you when the beta opens. Email avzrule@gmail.com to be removed.

How photos are processed

To identify and price an item, your photo and hint are sent to our server (hosted on Supabase) and then to Anthropic's Claude API, which analyzes the image and searches public sale listings. Anthropic processes this data under its commercial API terms and does not use it to train models. Photos are stored in your private history until you delete the scan or your account.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell your data.
  • We do not show third-party advertising.
  • We do not use your photos for any purpose other than producing your estimate and history.

Service providers

  • Supabase — hosting, database, image storage, authentication.
  • Anthropic — AI image analysis and price research.
  • RevenueCat — subscription management.
  • Apple App Store / Google Play — payment processing.

Retention and deletion

Scans stay in your history until you delete them. You can delete your entire account and all associated data at any time from Settings → Delete account. Deletion is immediate and permanent.

Children

Resalio is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their data.

Changes

If this policy changes, the effective date above will be updated and material changes will be noted in the app.
